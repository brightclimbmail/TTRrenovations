How it works

A clear path from first call to final walkthrough.

Renovations go best when nobody's guessing. Here's what working with us looks like.

  1. 01

    Initial Conversation

    Tell us about your home and what you have in mind. We'll talk through goals, timing and a realistic budget range.

  2. 02

    Walkthrough & Scope

    We visit the house, take measurements, look at what's behind the walls where we can, and define the scope together.

  3. 03

    Detailed Proposal

    A written proposal and contract that spells out the work, materials, schedule and payment terms. No vague line items.

  4. 04

    Planning & Scheduling

    Selections are finalized, materials ordered, permits handled where required, and trades scheduled in the right order.

  5. 05

    Build & Stay in Touch

    Work happens on a clear schedule, with regular updates and a protected, organized job site.

  6. 06

    Final Walkthrough

    We walk the finished space with you, close out any punch-list items, and make sure you're genuinely happy.

Good questions

Before you call.

Answers to what homeowners usually ask first. Don't see yours? Give us a call.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Brookfield and work primarily within about 30 miles: Brookfield, New Milford, New Fairfield, Danbury, Bethel, Newtown, Ridgefield, Redding and the surrounding communities. For larger projects we also travel to towns like Wilton, Weston, Easton, New Canaan, Westport. If you're not sure, ask.

Do you do electrical and plumbing work?

Yes, as part of your project. Electrical and plumbing work is performed by appropriately licensed trade partners, and we coordinate their schedules so you have one point of contact.

Will you pull the permits?

Yes. When your project requires permits, TTR pulls them. We'll explain what's needed before any work begins.

What happens after I request an estimate?

We'll reach out to learn more about your project and, if it's a good fit, schedule a time to walk through your home together.

What does TTR Renovations do?

TTR Renovations is a residential remodeling company based in Brookfield, Connecticut. We handle kitchen and bathroom remodels, basement finishing, whole-home renovations, carpentry, masonry and exterior work for homeowners in Brookfield, New Milford, New Fairfield, Danbury, Bethel, Newtown and nearby Western Connecticut towns.
